About

rana. was founded in 2020. The studio designs residential and commercial interiors with natural, timeless elements, with projects across Jakarta, Tangerang and West Java.

In Jakarta and Tangerang Selatan the work is design-and-build. Across the rest of Indonesia, it is design only.

Services
